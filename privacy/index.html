Privacy

What Later Love UK collects about visitors (almost nothing) and how affiliate links handle cookies.

Later Love UK doesn’t use analytics, advertising trackers or sign-up forms, and doesn’t ask for or store any personal information.

What this site stores

  • Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Fonts are served from this site; photos are loaded from Unsplash, which sees a normal request for each image.

Affiliate links

If you click an affiliate link, the affiliate network and the dating service may set cookies to record that you came from this site. That happens on their sites, under their privacy policies, not this one.
