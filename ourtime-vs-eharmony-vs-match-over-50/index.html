OurTime vs eharmony vs Match for Over-50s (UK)

OurTime, eharmony and Match compared for UK over-50s: prices, who's on each, what's free, and which suits you. Prices checked October 2026.

Bala Danmeri

Short answer: choose OurTime if you want a site where nearly everyone is over 45. Choose Match for the biggest UK membership and the cheapest six-month plan. Choose eharmony if you want guided matching for a long-term relationship and can commit to six months or more. None of them lets you message freely without paying.

All prices below are from DatingScout’s UK price checks in October 2026.1 Prices change and checkout offers vary, so confirm on the site before you pay.

Membership numbers are each site’s own claims, as reported by DatingScout, and they count everyone who ever registered.2 Active members are far fewer on every site.

OurTime: built for over-50s

OurTime’s selling point is simple: you’re not wading through profiles of people decades younger. DatingScout estimates about 55% of its UK members are 55 or over, and the rest mostly 45 to 54.

The downsides are well documented. Its main Trustpilot page scores 1.3 out of 5, with complaints about inactive profiles and billing. It’s also the only one of the three with a monthly price above £30. The full OurTime review covers who it suits.

Match: biggest and cheapest over six months

Match has the most claimed UK members of the general sites here, and its six-month plan works out at £9.99 a month, the lowest in this comparison.3

Free members can create a profile, search and send likes, and can talk to members who’ve paid for Connect. The catch is that Match is for all ages, so you’ll set your age filters and see a wider mix. It’s also owned by Match Group, the same company as OurTime.

eharmony: guided matching, longer commitment

eharmony matches people on a detailed compatibility questionnaire rather than leaving you to browse, and it’s aimed at long-term relationships. DatingScout reports a slightly female-leaning membership, about 53% women.

Its plans start at six months, so it has the highest upfront cost here, although longer plans bring the monthly price down.4 Free members can’t see photos or message freely.

Which should you pick?

  • You want people your own age and you’ll message first: OurTime.
  • You’re watching the cost: Match’s six-month plan.
  • You want a serious, long-term match and don’t mind the commitment: eharmony.
  • You’re not sure: make free profiles on two of them. A free profile costs nothing, and seeing who’s actually near you is the best test.

Whichever you choose, read the romance scam warning signs before you start messaging, and note your renewal date the day you pay.
