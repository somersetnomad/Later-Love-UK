**Short answer:** as of October 2026, OurTime UK costs **£39.99 for one month**, **£74.97 for three months** (£24.99 a month) or **£119.94 for six months** (£19.99 a month).[^1] Every plan **renews automatically**. To stop it, cancel the renewal **at least 48 hours before your renewal date**, in the same place you paid: the website, the App Store, or Google Play.

### Extras that cost more

On top of a subscription, OurTime sells optional extras:[^2]

- **Incognito mode:** £1.99 a day, or £9.99 for a month
- **Boosts**, which push your profile up the list: from £1.99 for one
- **Love Notes:** from £7.99 for five

None of these are needed to message people. They're the most common way the cost creeps up, so decide your budget before you start.

## The cheapest way to try it

This is where the plan prices hide a trap and a saving.

- **If you'll use it for just one month**, the one-month plan at £39.99 is the cheapest option.
- **If you might use it for two months**, the three-month plan (£74.97) costs less than paying for one month twice (£79.98), and gives you a spare month.
- **If you're committed for three months or more**, the six-month plan works out at half the monthly price of the one-month plan.

A sensible route is to browse free first, which costs nothing.[^3] If you then like who you're seeing nearby, pay for **one month**, and decide in week three whether to carry on.

## How to cancel OurTime UK

Cancel in the same place you paid. OurTime's help pages give these routes:[^4]

**On a computer**
1. Log in and open your account settings.
2. Go to **Manage account**, then **Manage subscription**.
3. Cancel the automatic renewal and follow it through to the end.

**On a phone (mobile site or Android app)**
1. Go to your profile, then **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **Preferences** and choose **Cancel your pass**.
3. Complete the cancellation.

**If you paid through Google Play**, open the Play Store, tap your profile, then **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**, and cancel OurTime there.

**If you paid through an iPhone**, open the iPhone's **Settings**, tap your name, then **Subscriptions**, and cancel OurTime there.

### Get the timing right

OurTime says you can cancel **no earlier than 24 hours after you buy** and **no later than 48 hours before the renewal date**. Miss that window and you'll pay for another full term.

The day you subscribe, put a reminder in your phone or calendar for **three days before** the renewal date.

### Why some people are still charged after cancelling

OurTime's own help page lists the usual reasons:[^5]

- **You suspended your profile instead of cancelling.** Suspending hides you but doesn't stop the subscription renewing. This is the most common mix-up.
- **The cancellation didn't finish.** If you didn't get a confirmation email, it may not have gone through.
- **You have a second profile** with its own subscription.
- **You bought extras** that are billed separately.
- **You paid in instalments**, in which case payments can still be due after you cancel.

If you're charged after cancelling properly, keep your confirmation email and contact OurTime's customer service. If that doesn't resolve it, ask your bank or card provider about disputing the payment.

## Refunds

DatingScout reports that OurTime UK offers a 14-day money-back guarantee.[^6] OurTime's own help pages don't spell out the terms, so if you change your mind soon after paying, contact customer service straight away and ask in writing.

## Questions people ask

### How much is OurTime UK per month?

£39.99 for a single month, £24.99 a month on the three-month plan, or £19.99 a month on the six-month plan, as of October 2026.

### How do I cancel OurTime UK?

In account settings, then Manage account, then Manage subscription. If you paid through the App Store or Google Play, cancel in that store instead.

### When do I need to cancel by?

No later than 48 hours before your renewal date.

### Why am I still being charged after cancelling?

Usually because the profile was suspended rather than the renewal cancelled. Suspending doesn't stop payments.
