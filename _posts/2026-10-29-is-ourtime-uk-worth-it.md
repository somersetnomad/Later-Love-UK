## Who uses OurTime UK

OurTime is aimed at people over 50, and its membership reflects that. DatingScout, which reviewed the UK site this month, puts the split at roughly 45% aged 45–54 and 55% aged 55 and over.[^2]

The headline figure is about **2 million registered UK members**, but that's the site's own claim. DatingScout's estimate of people actually using it is far lower: around 100,000 active UK members, with about 175,000 using it in a typical week. The gender split leans male, at about 60% men to 40% women.

For a woman over 50, that ratio means plenty of interest. For a man, it means more competition, and it makes a good profile and photo more important.

## What you get for free

OurTime's help pages say a free account lets you create a profile, see a selection of profiles, and look at up to six singles in your search at a time.[^3] DatingScout adds that free members can like profiles and use Shuffle, which shows a stream of profiles to say yes or no to.

What free members **can't** do is the part that matters: send messages. You also can't see who has liked or viewed you.

## What a subscription unlocks

According to OurTime, paying gets you:

- unlimited messaging, and access to messages you've received
- a list of who has liked and viewed your profile
- the ability to go back to profiles you've seen
- no adverts
- advanced search filters and a few Super Likes a week, with the upgraded profile

Some extras cost more on top of a subscription, including profile Boosts and Private Mode.[^4] Current prices, and how to cancel, are in the [cost and cancellation guide]({{ '/ourtime-uk-cost-and-how-to-cancel/' | relative_url }}).

## What members complain about

OurTime's main Trustpilot page has a TrustScore of **1.3 out of 5** from more than 2,100 reviews, with 53% of them one-star.[^5] The complaints fall into a few clear groups:

- **Fake or inactive profiles.** Members describe likes and views from people who never reply, or profiles that disappear.
- **Billing.** Some say they were charged after they thought they'd cancelled.
- **Cancelling and deleting accounts.** Several found it hard to cancel or to remove their profile.
- **Customer service.** Many say emails and calls went unanswered.

Reviews are self-selected, and unhappy customers are more likely to write one. But the same themes appear again and again, so it's worth planning around them rather than hoping you'll be the exception.

### The billing complaints, explained

OurTime's own help pages explain the most common billing surprise. **Suspending your profile does not cancel your subscription**, so it keeps renewing until you cancel the renewal itself.[^6] The [cancellation guide]({{ '/ourtime-uk-cost-and-how-to-cancel/' | relative_url }}) walks through the right steps for the website, iPhone and Android.

## Safety features

OurTime lets you report any profile, or anyone in your messages, from the three-dot menu, and its moderation team can remove members.[^7] You can also earn a Verified badge by uploading a selfie, which is worth doing yourself and looking for in others.

No site can screen out every bad actor. Before you message anyone, read the [romance scam warning signs]({{ '/romance-scams-over-50-warning-signs/' | relative_url }}). People aged 55 to 74 lose more to romance fraud than any other age group.

## Who it suits, and who should skip it

**It may suit you if:**

- you want to meet people your own age, without younger users in the mix
- you're comfortable messaging first rather than waiting to be contacted
- you'll use it steadily for a month or two, then decide

**Skip it, or wait, if:**

- you'd rather not pay before you can say hello. Match lets free members talk to people who've paid for a feature called Connect. See the [comparison of OurTime, eharmony and Match]({{ '/ourtime-vs-eharmony-vs-match-over-50/' | relative_url }}).

## Questions people ask

### Is OurTime UK free?

You can join, make a profile and browse a limited selection for free. Messaging, reading messages, and seeing who liked or viewed you all need a subscription.

### Who owns OurTime UK?

Match Group, which also owns Match.com.

### Does OurTime UK have fake profiles?

Many reviewers say so. OurTime checks profiles and photos and offers a selfie Verified badge, but fake or inactive profiles are the most common complaint.

### Does OurTime renew automatically?

Yes. Cancel the renewal no later than 48 hours before the renewal date, or you'll be charged for another term.
