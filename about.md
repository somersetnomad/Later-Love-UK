I'm Bala Danmeri. I write from Somerset, where I also write science fiction and metaphysical philosophy and trade as Noor Intelligence.

Later Love UK exists because the honest answers about dating later in life are hard to find. Search for the best dating site for over-50s and most of what comes back is a ranking built around commission. Prices, renewal terms, cancellation steps and the real risks are scattered across terms pages, review sites and police advice.

## How the guides are written

- Every factual claim links to its source: the dating sites' own help pages, published price checks, police and fraud-reporting services, and independent safety charities.
- Prices change often, so each guide shows the date it was last checked. Always confirm the price on the site before you pay.
- Member complaints are reported as they are, including on sites this site links to.
- Safety comes first. If something in a guide could put you at risk, the guide says so plainly.
